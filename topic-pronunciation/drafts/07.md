# Nasal vowels: say it through your nose
- Some air goes out through your nose.
- Spelled vowel + n or m, but you **don't** say the n.
- No nasal if a vowel follows: bon (nasal) vs bonne (not nasal).
- ⚠ 3 or 4? Standard French has up to 4, but around Paris most people merge un into in, so you often hear 3.
🔊 beau ≈ boh /bo/
🔊 bon ≈ bohn /bɔ̃/
🔊 vin ≈ va(n) /vɛ̃/
Notes: Pinch your nose on "bon": you should feel it buzz.
