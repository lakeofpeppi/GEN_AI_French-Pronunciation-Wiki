# Liaison: linking words together
- In certain spots, a silent final consonant wakes up before a vowel. s and x sound like z.
- **Always** link: determiner + noun, pronoun + verb.
- **Never** link after et ("and"). Some H words block it too.
- ⚠ Optional liaisons sound formal; beginners can skip them.
🔊 les amis ≈ lay-za-MEE /lezami/
🔊 vous avez ≈ voo-za-VAY /vuzave/
Notes: Contrast les amis (link) with les copains (no vowel, no link).
