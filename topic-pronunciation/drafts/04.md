# Rule 2: the disappearing e
- A final -e is usually silent, unless it's the only vowel (je, que).
- But it makes the consonant before it heard: petit vs petite.
- Unstressed e in the middle of a word often vanishes too. It's called the "mute e" (e muet).
🔊 petite ≈ puh-TEET /pətit/
🔊 je ≈ zhuh /ʒə/
🔊 appeler ≈ ap-LAY /aple/
Notes: Count syllables: appeler is written with 3 vowels but often said with 2.
