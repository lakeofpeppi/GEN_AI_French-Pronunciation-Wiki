# Top 4 beginner mistakes
1. Adding an "n" after a nasal vowel: bon is not "bonn".
2. Saying u like "oo": tu ≠ tout.
3. Pronouncing every final letter: parlent is said "parl", the -ent is silent.
4. Linking after et: Paul et Anne has no t.
🔊 Paul et Anne ≈ pol ay ahn /pɔl e an/
