# Reading French = patterns + IPA
- Learn the spelling patterns in this deck.
- Check new words in IPA: one symbol per sound.
- Symbols you'll meet: /y/ (u), /ʁ/ (r), and nasal vowels with a tilde like /ɔ̃/.
🔊 bon ≈ bohn /bɔ̃/
🔊 rue ≈ rü /ʁy/
Notes: Respellings in this deck are rough English hints; IPA is the precise version.
