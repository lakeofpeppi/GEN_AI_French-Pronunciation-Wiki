# What accents do
- **é** = "ay": été.
- **ç** (cedilla) makes c sound like s.
- **à / où**: the grave accent just tells words apart.
- **ê, ô** (circumflex): often marks a lost s (fête ← feste); doesn't always change the sound.
🔊 été ≈ ay-TAY /ete/
🔊 garçon ≈ gar-SOHN /ɡaʁsɔ̃/
Notes: Keep accents when writing; they carry information.
