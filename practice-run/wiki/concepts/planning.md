# Planning (規劃)

**One line:** make the LLM think or plan before it answers.

## Explanation
Asking the model to plan first (list steps, or reason out loud) breaks a complex question
into parts, shows how they relate, and covers the problem more completely.
Chain-of-thought is the best-known form.

## Key points
- Ask the LLM to "think" or draft a plan before the real answer [L08 p.32]
- Chain-of-thought breaks complex problems down systematically [L08 p.34]

## Example
"Before answering, list the steps you'll take. Then do them one by one."
