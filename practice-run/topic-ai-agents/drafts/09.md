# Hand over work like a good boss
Tell the agent three things:
- **Result:** what you get, where, in what format.
- **Limits:** what may and may not be changed.
- **Done:** how you will check it's finished.
A loop spec adds two more: when to **stop** and what **triggers** it.
Notes: The #1 agent failure is guessing. These three remove the guessing.
