## Concepts
- [[spelling-vs-sound]] — letters don't map one-to-one to sounds
- [[silent-letters]] — final consonants, -e and -ent are usually silent
- [[nasal-vowels]] — air through the nose, no "n" sound
- [[front-rounded-vowels]] — the u sound: "ee" with rounded lips
- [[french-r]] — throaty r at the back of the mouth
- [[accents]] — é, è, ç and friends
- [[liaison]] — a silent final consonant links to the next vowel
- [[enchainement]] — an already-pronounced consonant slides onto the next word
- [[intonation]] — pitch falls for statements, rises for yes/no questions
- [[vowel-spellings]] — ai, eau, ou, oi and other vowel combinations
- [[consonant-spellings]] — h, j, ch, qu, th
- [[schwa]] — the unstable "mute e" that often disappears
- [[stress]] — even syllables, light stress at the end of a word group
- [[ipa]] — one symbol per sound; how to read /bɔ̃/
