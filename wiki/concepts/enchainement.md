# Enchaînement

**One line:** a final consonant you *already* pronounce slides onto the next word's vowel.

## Explanation
Unlike liaison, nothing silent wakes up: the consonant is always pronounced, it just moves
over to start the next syllable, so the words flow without a break.

## Key points
- The consonant is already pronounced and carried over. [S1 §5. Liaison or enchaînement?] «Already pronounced; carried into the next vowel»
- In bel homme the l is just carried over. [S1 §5. Liaison or enchaînement?] «In bel, you already pronounce the l»

## Examples
- `bel homme` ≈ beh-LOM /bɛlɔm/
