# The u sound (front rounded vowels)

**One line:** French u is "ee" said with rounded lips, different from "oo" (ou).

## Explanation
English has no vowel like French u /y/. You make it by putting your tongue where it goes for
"ee" and rounding your lips as for "oo". Mixing up u and ou changes the word.

## Key points
- Tell tu and tout apart. [S1 §Open and closed vowels] «Also distinguish tu /ty/ and tout /tu/»
- Start from /i/ and round your lips. [S1 §Open and closed vowels] «begin with the tongue position of /i/ and round your lips»
- Say "ee" with a kissy face. [S2 §Vowel spellings and pronunciation] «say “ee” like in English see while rounding your lips»

## Examples
- `tu` ≈ tü (ee + round lips) /ty/ vs `tout` ≈ too /tu/
