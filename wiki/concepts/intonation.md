# Intonation

**One line:** the rise and fall of pitch across a sentence.

## Explanation
Pitch carries meaning in French sentences: the same words can be a statement or a question
depending on whether your voice falls or rises at the end.

## Key points
- Statements fall in pitch. [S1 §Liaison and intonation] «Statements generally end with a falling pitch»
- Yes/no questions often rise. [S1 §Liaison and intonation] «yes/no questions formed by intonation often rise»

## Examples
- `Tu viens.` (falls) vs `Tu viens ?` (rises) ≈ tü vyeh /ty vjɛ̃/
