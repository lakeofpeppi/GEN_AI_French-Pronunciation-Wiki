# Vowel spellings

**One line:** French writes many vowel sounds with letter pairs (ai, eau, ou, oi).

## Explanation
Several French vowel sounds are spelled with two or three letters. Once you know the common
combinations, you can read most words aloud correctly.

## Key points
- Some vowel letters have more than one sound. [S2 §Vowel spellings and pronunciation] «have multiple pronunciations, how do you know which one to use?»
- The syllable type decides which one. [S2 §Vowel spellings and pronunciation] «The answer is in the type of syllable those vowels appear in!»
- /ɔ/ sounds like "aw" in saw. [S2 §Vowel spellings and pronunciation] «makes the same sound as “aw” in American English saw»
- /œ/ sounds like the "i" in sir. [S2 §Vowel spellings and pronunciation] «sounds like “i” in English sir»

## Examples
- 🔊 `bonjour` ≈ bohn-ZHOOR /bɔ̃ʒuʁ/ · `moi` ≈ mwa /mwa/ · `beau` ≈ boh /bo/
