# Consonant spellings

**One line:** most consonants sound like English, with a few key differences (h, j, ch, qu, th).

## Explanation
Many consonant letters already work like English. The main traps: h is silent, j sounds like
the s in "pleasure", ch sounds like "sh", qu sounds like k, and th sounds like t.

## Key points
- h is always silent. [S2 §What sounds do French consonants make?] «“h” is always silent in French»
- j sounds like the s in pleasure. [S2 §What sounds do French consonants make?] «“j” sounds like the “s” in English pleasure»

## Examples
- `hôtel` ≈ oh-TEL /otɛl/ · `je` ≈ zhuh /ʒə/
