# S4 — Help:IPA/French (Wikipedia)

## What it covers
The key to IPA symbols for French, with example words and rough English equivalents, plus
notes on regional variation.

## Key points
- It's a symbol key for French pronunciation. [S4 §Help:IPA/French] «It provides a set of symbols to represent the pronunciation of French in Wikipedia»
- The French r is usually uvular, varying by region. [S4 §Notes] «The French rhotic /ʁ/ is usually guttural (uvular), but it varies by region»
- The merged pairs stay distinct outside Paris. [S4 §Notes] «These pairs are always distinguished in Northern, Belgian, Swiss, and Quebec French»
