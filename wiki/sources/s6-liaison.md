# S6 — Liaison (French) (Wikipedia)

## What it covers
A detailed account of liaison: where it is mandatory, optional or impossible, how linking
consonants sound, common errors, and its history.

## Key points
- Liaison only happens before a vowel or semivowel. [S6 §Liaison (French)] «Liaison only happens when the following word starts with a vowel or semivowel»
- Some contexts are truly mandatory. [S6 §Mandatory liaison] «These are the contexts where liaison is truly mandatory»
- Determiner + noun is mandatory. [S6 §Mandatory liaison] «between a determiner and a following adjective or noun»
- Pronoun + verb is mandatory. [S6 §Mandatory liaison] «between a subject or object pronoun and the verb»
- Optional liaisons sound formal. [S6 §Optional liaison] «The realization of optional liaisons is a signal of formal register»
- Never after et. [S6 §Impossible liaison] «after certain words, for example et ("and"), and all singular nouns»
