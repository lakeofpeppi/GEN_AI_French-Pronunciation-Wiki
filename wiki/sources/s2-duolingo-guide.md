# S2 — Dear Duolingo: How do you know what sounds French letters make? (2025)

## What it covers
Two PhD linguists explain French spelling-to-sound rules for learners: consonant letters,
vowel spellings, nasal vowels, word-final e combinations, and which final letters to say.

## Key points
- French pronunciation is predictable from spelling. [S2 §dear duolingo] «French pronunciation is actually predictable from spelling»
- h is always silent. [S2 §What sounds do French consonants make?] «“h” is always silent in French»
- Any r is understandable. [S2 §Hey, what about the French "r"?] «any “r” will do»
- French has 4 nasal vowel sounds. [S2 §Vowel + "m" or "n"] «French has 4 “nasal” vowel sounds»
- Final c, r, f, l are pronounced (CaReFuL). [S2 §What letters should or shouldn't be pronounced?] «If a word ends with consonants c, r, f, l»
