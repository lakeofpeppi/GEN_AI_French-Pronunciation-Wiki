# S5 — French orthography (Wikipedia)

## What it covers
How French is spelled and why: history of silent letters, the diacritics, and detailed
spelling-to-sound tables for consonants and vowels.

## Key points
- Latin-based spellings added many silent letters. [S5 §French orthography] «further increased the number of silent letters»
- é represents /e/. [S5 §Diacritics] «An acute accent over ⟨e⟩ represents /e/»
- The grave accent on a or u mostly tells homophones apart. [S5 §Diacritics] «is primarily used to distinguish homophones»
- A circumflex often marks a lost letter, usually s. [S5 §Diacritics] «it indicates the historical deletion of an adjacent letter»
- A cedilla makes c an s sound. [S5 §Diacritics] «A cedilla under ⟨c⟩ indicates that it is pronounced /s/ rather than /k/»
